About

what I actually do

I like being responsible for the whole request, not one slice of it.

I finished my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering in 2025 and spent most of it building things end to end — the interface, the service behind it, and the model or database it leans on. Java and Python are where I'm most comfortable; JavaScript, HTML and CSS are how I get it in front of someone.

The projects I keep coming back to are detection problems: tuberculosis from scans, disease in a plant leaf, sign language from a camera, whether a donor gives again. Same shape every time — get the data honest, train something on it, then wrap it in an interface a person can actually use.
